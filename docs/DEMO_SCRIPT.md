# Judge presentation — about four minutes

1. Open the game and choose **Play solo demo**. No account, key, or second player is needed. Three witnesses are explicitly marked scripted.
2. Move left with A for roughly half a second. Press E by the badge scanner. Its audit record is shared evidence.
3. Press M. Read the first memory, choose a confidence stake, and share it. Your recollection puts the arrival in a different lab from two scripted witnesses. Close with Escape.
4. Press T. Compare the highlighted location clash. Believe, doubt, or mark a contradiction. A majority can accept the wrong memory; the Archivist warns without identifying it.
5. Explore the north labs or south recorders. Other badges recover records you cannot unlock. Enter opens room radio with quick reactions. The gear controls sound and reduced motion.
6. Press Tab to reconstruct. Select a record, then choose a position, or drag it onto the sequence. Evidence is reliable; confidence is not. Any edit clears votes. Scripted witnesses propose a sequence around two minutes if you have not filled it.
7. Vote to lock. Once three witnesses agree, the camera visits six moments over twenty-six seconds. Human input cannot change the locked record. Without a human vote the rehearsal closes automatically around three minutes.
8. Review Reality Recovered, the group archetype, confidence mistakes, and belief spread. Download the portrait share card or share the saved replay. Rematch starts a new factual variant.

For a group demonstration, create a room and open the invite in three separate tabs or devices. Each tab owns its own reconnect identity. All four must ready; the host begins. Physical records require different badge groups, so compare discoveries over radio.

Avoid spoilers when presenting: **clear** describes how a memory feels, not whether it is true. The first case is authored and repeatable; later rounds vary facts.
